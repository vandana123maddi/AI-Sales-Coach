# Real-estate conversation research for AI Sales Coach

## Buyer signals and questions

Buyers commonly ask about location, school catchment, commute, bedrooms, condition, service charges, energy performance, viewing availability, competing offers, and offer process. Qualification should establish intended use, budget range, deposit/financing status, move timeline, decision makers, and non-negotiables. Serious signals include a mortgage agreement in principle, specific availability, a defined target area, and willingness to view; casual signals include broad browsing, no timeframe, or an unqualified budget.

Typical concerns are affordability, mortgage approval, hidden repair costs, chain risk, valuation fairness, and whether the property suits family or work needs. The coach should acknowledge the stated concern, ask one discriminating question, and propose an appropriate next action: refine criteria, arrange a viewing, introduce a mortgage adviser, or prepare an offer.

## Vendor signals and questions

Vendors ask about valuation, asking price versus achieved price, time on market, commission and contract terms, marketing plan, viewings, competing agents, and whether to accept an offer. Urgency signals include a chain deadline, relocation, probate, financial pressure, or an imminent onward purchase. Instruction signals include sharing property details, asking for a valuation appointment, or comparing agent terms.

The coach should not assert a price without evidence. It should recommend a valuation discussion, explain the evidence needed for pricing, ask about timescale and onward plans, and handle fee objections by reconnecting fee to marketing, negotiation, and qualification value.

## Progression and next moves

| Stage | Useful next move |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery | Clarify property type, area, reason for moving, and decision makers. |
| Qualification | Confirm budget, financing, timeline, chain, and viewing availability. |
| Objection handling | Name the concern, ask a focused follow-up, and offer evidence or options. |
| Viewing | Confirm availability, requirements, and what would make a viewing worthwhile. |
| Offer | Check readiness, conditions, funding, chain, and propose a clear next step. |
| Valuation/instruction | Establish goals, property facts, market evidence, fees, and appointment. |
| Closing/follow-up | Recap the agreed action, owner, timing, and communication channel. |

## How this guides the product

The coach prompt requires a grounded stage, sentiment, and next-best move. A price objection should lead to budget flexibility or value evidence—not generic encouragement. A warm, qualified buyer should receive a viewing/offer action; a vendor requesting a valuation should receive a valuation appointment action. Summary fields preserve only transcript-supported objections, needs, commitments, and next steps.
